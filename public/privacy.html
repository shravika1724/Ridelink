Section 02

Categories of Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide when registering an account, submitting KYC documents, listing a vehicle, booking a rental, or communicating through the platform:

  • Account & Contact Details: Full legal name, verified email address, mobile phone number, account password (stored exclusively as salted one-way bcrypt cryptographic hashes), and avatar/profile picture.
  • Government KYC & Verification Documents: Driving License number (masked to display only prefix and last 4 digits in database records), Driving License document copy/PDF, Aadhaar number (masked in full compliance with UIDAI circulars e.g. XXXX-XXXX-1234), and Aadhaar document copy for identity verification.
  • Host Vehicle Information: Registration number (RC), vehicle make, model, manufacturing year, seating capacity, fuel type, transmission, vehicle photos, city location, and pickup address.
  • Host Banking & Payout Data: Account holder name, masked bank account number, IFSC code, bank branch name, and Virtual Payment Address (UPI ID) for crediting rental earnings.
  • Transactional & Booking Data: Rental start and end dates, pickup/return locations, trip fare breakdown, damage protection fees, 18% GST (CGST/SGST) records, Razorpay payment reference IDs, UPI transaction IDs, and tax invoice numbers. (Note: We never collect or store full credit/debit card numbers or CVV codes; all payment entry is handled securely by Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 gateway).
  • Trip Messaging & Reviews: Communications exchanged via the in-app trip messaging system between Renter and Host, trip ratings, and public vehicle reviews.
Section 03

Purposes for Which We Process Your Data

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate, contractual, and statutory purposes:

  • Trip Facilitation: Processing bookings, verifying vehicle availability, coordinating pickup and return schedules, and enabling communication between Host and Renter.
  • Identity & License Authentication: Verifying driving eligibility with MoRTH / DigiLocker sandbox APIs to ensure that only legally licensed drivers take custody of motor vehicles under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
  • Payment Processing & Invoicing: Authorizing rental payments, issuing GST tax invoices, holding refundable security deposits, and remitting payouts to vehicle hosts.
  • Safety & Fraud Prevention: Preventing identity theft, combating unauthorized vehicle listings, investigating accident claims, and managing damage reports.
  • Statutory Compliance: Complying with tax audit regulations under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) laws and lawful requests from Indian police or judicial authorities.
Section 04

How and When We Share Your Information

RideLink NEVER sells, rents, or monetizes your personal data to third-party marketing companies or data brokers. Data is shared exclusively under the following strict conditions:

  • Between Host and Renter: When a reservation is confirmed, we share the necessary contact details (first name, phone number, pickup address, and vehicle registration number) between the matched Host and Renter strictly to facilitate key handover and vehicle return.
  • Payment Gateway Partners: Transaction values and billing identifiers are shared with our authorized payment processor (Razorpay Software Private Limited) to execute encrypted payment authorization and refunds.
  • Law Enforcement & Legal Compliance: We may disclose user details when mandated by a formal written notice, court warrant, or statutory order under Section 91 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 / Section 94 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023.
Section 05

Data Security, Encryption & Masking Procedures

We employ comprehensive technical and organizational security measures to protect your data from loss, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

Bank-Grade Protection Architecture
  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) with 256-bit certificates.
  • Password Security: Passwords are never stored in plaintext; they are irreversibly hashed using salted bcrypt (cost factor 10).
  • Masked Government IDs: Aadhaar and Driving License numbers are masked prior to database persistence (e.g. XXXX-XXXX-7842 and DL-04-2019-XXXX-4819), preventing exposure even during administrative reviews.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Backend database access is strictly partitioned according to verified user roles (Customer, Owner, Administrator).
Section 06

Data Retention Periods & Account Deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered, or to comply with statutory accounting and tax retention periods under Indian law:

  • Active Accounts: Profile data, vehicle listings, and active booking records are retained for the active duration of your account.
  • Statutory Tax Records: Invoices, transaction codes, and GST breakdown records must be preserved for at least 8 years pursuant to Section 36 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017.
  • Account Deletion: You may request account deletion at any time by contacting support. Upon receipt, your personal profile and uploaded KYC documents will be purged, subject to statutory retention of past financial transaction logs.
Section 07

Your Rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

As a Data Principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you are entitled to the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can view your stored profile details, active bookings, transactions, and vehicle listings directly via your user dashboard.
  • Right to Correction & Updation: You may correct inaccurate or incomplete profile data, vehicle descriptions, or bank account details at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request deletion of personal information where processing is no longer necessary for contractual or statutory compliance.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to readily accessible grievance redressal mechanisms regarding personal data processing.
Section 08

Cookies, Web Tokens & Local Storage

RideLink utilizes standard browser storage technologies to deliver a fast, responsive, and secure experience:

  • Authentication Tokens: We store a JSON Web Token (ridelink_token) in your browser's local storage to maintain your logged-in session securely across page visits.
  • Preferences: Basic display preferences and cached profile data (ridelink_user) are stored locally to prevent repeated network queries.

You can clear browser local storage or disable cookies at any time in your browser settings; however, doing so will log you out of your account session.

Section 09

Children's Privacy & Age Restrictions

RideLink provides motor vehicle rental services that require a valid government driving license and legal contractual capacity. Consequently, our Platform is strictly restricted to individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect, request, or maintain personal data from minors. If we discover that a minor under 18 has submitted personal data, we will immediately delete the account and purge associated data.
