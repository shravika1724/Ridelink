Section 09

Vehicle Pickup, Handover & Return Protocol

To prevent disputes regarding pre-existing blemishes versus new trip damages, Host and Renter must adhere to the joint inspection protocol:

  • Pre-Trip Joint Inspection: Before keys are handed over, both parties must inspect the exterior and interior, record the exact odometer reading and fuel level, and take timestamped 360-degree photos of any scratches, dents, or defects.
  • Return Location & Time: The vehicle must be returned to the agreed pickup location (or doorstep address if booked with delivery add-on) on or before the scheduled return timestamp.
  • Post-Trip Sign-off: The Host will inspect the vehicle upon return, check fuel level, and confirm the trip completion in the app. The security deposit refund is triggered upon successful return sign-off.

Prohibited Vehicle Activities

The vehicle must NOT be used under any circumstances for the following prohibited activities:
